Why curb appeal pays

The front yard is your listing's first photo.

Buyers scroll past a tired exterior before they ever see your new kitchen. A clean, green, finished yard gets them in the door, and the numbers back it up.

  • 92%of Realtors suggest sellers improve curb appeal before listing
  • 100%of the cost of an overall landscape upgrade is estimated to be recovered at sale
  • 104%of the cost of landscape maintenance is estimated to be recovered at sale

Source: National Association of Realtors & NALP, 2023 Remodeling Impact Report: Outdoor Features
